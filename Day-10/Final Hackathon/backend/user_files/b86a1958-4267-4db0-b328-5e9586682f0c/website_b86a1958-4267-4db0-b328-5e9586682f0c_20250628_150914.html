How to Cook Delicious Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg Basmati rice
  • 500g Chicken/Lamb/Vegetables
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Spices (turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala)
  • Yogurt
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Oil/Ghee

Instructions:

  1. Wash and soak the rice for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Marinate the meat/vegetables with yogurt and spices.
  3. Sauté onions until golden brown.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and spices, cook for a minute.
  5. Add marinated meat/vegetables and cook until tender.
  6. Layer rice and meat/vegetable mixture in a pot.
  7. Add water and bring to a boil, then simmer until rice is cooked.
  8. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
  9. Serve hot!
